# Launch your dPERPS app with AI

Your assistant customizes the [official trading app](https://github.com/orbs-network/perpetual-hub-frontend) and publishes it in your hosting account. Allow roughly **30–60 minutes**; first-time installs and team approval can take longer. You do not need a GitHub account or coding experience.

On a computer, choose **Launch your DEX in 2 minutes** in the portal and copy the launch instructions. On a phone, choose **Copy launch instructions** and continue in your desktop coding assistant. It carries your requested Partner ID, project name, networks, permanent folder and activation contact. The assistant reuses your customized copy or clones the official repository, then explicitly reads `AGENTS.md` and [`.agents/skills/partner-launch/SKILL.md`](https://github.com/orbs-network/perpetual-hub-frontend/blob/main/.agents/skills/partner-launch/SKILL.md). The prompt describes the requested launch flow; the repository supplies implementation, build and verification instructions for its current code. No separate skill download is needed.

You’ll grant access to the permanent folder and first provide a logo file or its local path, or choose text-only branding. The assistant then asks separately about dark/light appearance and your primary color. Branding is applied before the actual app preview, which stays available for adjustments. Review the design before Netlify setup; then choose an available `.netlify.app` name or use a generated address. After you accept the design, the assistant completes publication and verifies the stable URL. It handles routine technical choices on the checked-out branch and keeps explanations short. Passwords, tokens and private keys stay out of chat. The assistant checks the current Netlify account and terms before using free hosting, and asks before spending, upgrades, automatic recharge or DNS changes. Custom-domain purchases and DNS setup are outside this launch. A missing WalletConnect project ID does not block publication; an existing partner-owned ID is preserved, while optional setup instructions can wait until handoff. Use an empty wallet for an optional connection check, or skip it.

The assistant leaves your customized app, a private source backup and a project-local `DEPLOYMENT.md` with your verified hosting site, link, checks, remaining team work and a ready-to-paste update prompt. Future updates reuse the same folder and hosting site.

**Website published** and **Trading activation pending** are separate. The team must confirm the assigned Partner ID and activate the selected networks. A missing team contact or unavailable partner does not stop publishing. Until team activation, the branded application keeps its permanent coming-soon notice visible, hides wallet/chain controls and disables trading. Requested display metadata does not confirm network support. Your assistant should finish publication where possible and report what still needs the team, without asking you to verify team-only facts.

For an existing app or server instead of a hosted frontend, use [API getting started](https://docs.orbs.com/perps/api).
